# How to Get Clients for AI Services

A practical playbook for landing your first (and next) AI consulting, automation, or productized service clients.

---

## 1. Pick a narrow offer

Broad "AI services" is hard to sell. Clients buy outcomes, not models.

**Strong offers:**
- AI chatbots for customer support (reduce ticket volume by X%)
- Document processing / data extraction pipelines
- Internal knowledge-base search (RAG over company docs)
- Sales outreach automation (personalized email at scale)
- Custom dashboards / reporting with LLM summaries
- Workflow automation (Zapier/Make + AI steps)

**Formula:** *"I help [specific audience] achieve [measurable outcome] using [AI/automation]."*

Example: *"I help e-commerce brands cut support costs by 40% with AI chatbots trained on their product catalog."*

---

## 2. Build proof before you pitch

You need 1–3 case studies, even if they're self-initiated.

| Proof type | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Demo project** | Build a working prototype for a fake or real niche (e.g., a chatbot for a local gym) |
| **Free pilot** | Offer 2-week pilot to 1–2 businesses in exchange for testimonial + metrics |
| **Your own workflow** | Automate something in your business and document before/after |
| **Open-source / content** | Ship a small tool or write a teardown showing your process |

Always capture: problem → solution → result (time saved, cost cut, revenue up).

---

## 3. Where to find clients

### Warm network (fastest)
- Former colleagues, managers, founders you know
- LinkedIn connections in industries you've worked in
- Slack/Discord communities you're already active in

**Message template:**
> Hey [Name] — I've been building AI automations for [niche]. I noticed [specific pain point in their business/industry]. Would you be open to a 15-min call? Happy to share what's working for similar companies, no pitch unless it's a fit.

### Outbound (scalable)
- **LinkedIn:** Target ops managers, founders, heads of support at 10–200 person companies
- **Cold email:** Short, specific, one clear outcome; follow up 2–3 times
- **Local businesses:** Restaurants, clinics, agencies — often underserved and eager for simple wins

### Inbound (slower but compounding)
- Write LinkedIn posts / short videos showing real builds (before/after screens)
- Niche newsletter or blog ("AI for [industry]")
- Speak at local meetups or webinars
- List on Upwork/Toptal *only* with a sharp niche profile, not generic "AI expert"

### Partnerships
- Web dev agencies (they build sites, you add AI layer)
- Marketing agencies (you handle automation, they keep the client relationship)
- IT consultants / MSPs serving SMBs

Offer 10–20% referral fee or white-label delivery.

---

## 4. Pricing models

| Model | Best for | Typical range |
|---|---|---|
| **Project fee** | One-off builds (chatbot, integration) | $3k–$25k+ |
| **Retainer** | Ongoing maintenance, tuning, new features | $1.5k–$10k/mo |
| **Productized service** | Fixed scope, fixed price, repeatable | $500–$5k/mo |
| **Revenue share** | High-trust, measurable upside | % of savings or new revenue |

Start with project or productized pricing until you know delivery cost. Never charge hourly without a cap early on.

---

## 5. Sales process that works

1. **Discovery call (15–30 min)** — Understand pain, current tools, budget signals, timeline
2. **Scope doc (1 page)** — Problem, deliverables, timeline, price, what's *not* included
3. **50% upfront** — Standard for custom work; protects both sides
4. **Weekly updates** — Short Loom or email; builds trust
5. **Handoff + upsell** — Document everything; offer retainer for monitoring/improvements

**Red flags to avoid:**
- "We need AI but don't know why"
- No budget and no decision-maker on calls
- Scope creep with no change-order process

---

## 6. Stack & delivery (keep it simple)

Clients care about results, not your stack. Common reliable choices:

- **LLMs:** OpenAI, Anthropic, or open models via API
- **Automation:** n8n, Make, Zapier, or custom Python/Node
- **RAG:** Pinecone, Qdrant, pgvector, or Supabase
- **Hosting:** Railway, Fly.io, AWS/GCP for larger clients

Ship fast with off-the-shelf tools; custom code only where it adds clear value.

---

## 7. Content angles that attract clients

Post about **specific problems solved**, not "AI is the future."

- "How we cut invoice processing from 4 hours to 12 minutes"
- "The $200/mo AI stack this 20-person agency runs on"
- "Why your first chatbot failed (and the 3 fixes)"
- Live demos: screen recordings of automations running

One good post per week beats ten generic AI hype posts.

---

## 8. 30-day action plan

**Week 1:** Define niche + one offer + build one demo  
**Week 2:** Reach out to 20 warm contacts; post 2 pieces of proof content  
**Week 3:** 10 targeted outbound messages/day; book 3 discovery calls  
**Week 4:** Close 1 paid pilot or project; document case study  

Track in a simple spreadsheet: outreach → replies → calls → proposals → closes.

---

## 9. Metrics to watch

- **Reply rate** on outbound (aim for 5–15% with good targeting)
- **Call-to-close rate** (20–40% is healthy with qualified leads)
- **Average project value** (raise prices when close rate > 50%)
- **Referrals** (best signal you're delivering real value)
