## Positioning Takeaways For My Profile

1. Lead with a specific business outcome, not only technology.
   Example: `AI Automation Developer | Django, RAG, CRM Workflows, OpenAI Agents`.

2. Combine full-stack credibility with AI automation.
   This is stronger than only saying `AI developer`, because clients need working software, not just prompts.

3. Use keywords clients already search for.
   Include: `AI Agent Development`, `RAG`, `OpenAI API`, `n8n`, `API Integration`, `CRM Automation`, `Python`, `Django`, `React`, `PostgreSQL`.

4. Package services as concrete offers.
   Better offers: `AI chatbot over your company docs`, `CRM lead qualification automation`, `AI appointment assistant`, `internal knowledge base assistant`, `workflow automation with n8n + APIs`.

5. Portfolio should show business systems.
   Build or document 2-3 demo projects that look like client work: RAG company brain, AI lead qualifier, CRM/email automation, or AI assistant for support/operations.
