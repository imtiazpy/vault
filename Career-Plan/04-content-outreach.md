# Weekly Content + Outreach Plan

## Weekly operating rhythm

- **Monday:** publish LinkedIn post; send 10 cold emails/DMs and 3 warm messages.
- **Tuesday:** publish one useful Reddit comment/post; send 10 cold messages; follow up with last week's prospects.
- **Wednesday:** publish LinkedIn post and one X thread; send 10 cold messages and 3 warm messages.
- **Thursday:** publish a short YouTube video; send 10 cold messages; engage with people who commented or replied.
- **Friday:** publish LinkedIn post and an X post; send 10 cold messages, 3 warm messages, and follow up twice with qualified prospects.

Target: **3 LinkedIn posts, 3-5 X posts, 1 Reddit contribution, 1 YouTube video, 50 cold messages, and 9 warm messages each week.** Keep a simple sheet with prospect, channel, pain signal, send date, follow-up date, and reply status.

## LinkedIn

**Posting frequency:** 3 posts/week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday).

**Content angles:**

1. **The content-bottleneck teardown:** Show how a Django marketing site forces developers into routine copy, CTA, and landing-page edits; explain how reusable Wagtail blocks remove that queue.
2. **21-day launch breakdown:** Share a short week-by-week view of the CMS Blueprint, implementation, migration, staging, and handover in the Wagtail CMS Launch Sprint.
3. **Editor-ready proof:** Explain the “nominated editor updates a representative page” test and why an editor guide, permissions, and a recorded walkthrough matter more than a CMS handoff.

**Cold message:**

> Hi [Name] — I noticed [specific site/content signal]. I help Django teams move routine page updates out of the dev queue with a focused Wagtail CMS launch. Would it be useful if I sent you a 3-point idea for how I’d structure [Company]'s editable pages?

**Warm message (past client):**

> Hi [Name] — I enjoyed working together on [project]. I now offer a 21-day Wagtail CMS Launch Sprint for Django sites whose teams need to publish without developer help. Do you know anyone facing that bottleneck, or is it relevant to [Company]?

## Reddit

**Posting frequency:** 1 high-value contribution/week, plus 3-5 helpful comments. Prioritize relevant founder, Django, agency, startup, and marketing communities; follow each community's self-promotion rules and lead with the answer, not an offer.

**Content angles:**

1. **Decision guide:** “When does a Django site need Wagtail instead of another custom admin screen?” Include practical signals: recurring landing pages, non-technical editors, approvals, and reusable sections.
2. **Migration checklist:** Share a condensed pre-migration checklist: inventory priority pages, redirects, roles, reusable blocks, staging review, rollback, and editor training.
3. **Lessons from CMS handovers:** Explain the most common failure mode—shipping a CMS that developers understand but editors cannot use—and the acceptance test that prevents it.

**Cold message:**

> Hi [Name] — your post about [specific issue] caught my eye. I work on Django/Wagtail CMS migrations and may have a practical idea for reducing the content-update backlog. Want me to share it here?

**Warm message (past client):**

> Hi [Name] — quick update: I’m helping Django teams make their sites editable with Wagtail, including migration and editor handover. If a founder or agency in your network mentions being blocked by website changes, would you feel comfortable introducing us?

## X

**Posting frequency:** 3-5 posts/week: two standalone observations, one thread, and replies to 10 relevant founder/Django/agency posts across the week.

**Content angles:**

1. **Sharp pain-point posts:** “If every homepage edit becomes a developer ticket, your website is operating like software—not a marketing asset.” Follow with the Wagtail fix.
2. **Build-in-public snippets:** Share a blurred/sanitized content-model sketch or a before/after example of converting repeated hard-coded page sections into StreamField blocks.
3. **Scope clarity:** Explain what a $2,400 CMS launch covers—and why complex integrations, ecommerce, redesign, or broad migrations need separate scope.

**Cold message:**

> Hi [Name] — saw your post about [site/growth/content issue]. I help Django teams give marketers editable Wagtail pages without a full rebuild. Open to a quick 3-point suggestion for [Company]?

**Warm message (past client):**

> Hi [Name] — hope you’re well. I’ve packaged my Django/Wagtail work into a 21-day CMS launch for teams stuck waiting on developers for content changes. If anyone comes to mind, an intro would mean a lot.

## YouTube

**Posting frequency:** 1 concise video/week (4-8 minutes). Repurpose the key point as the week’s LinkedIn post and X thread.

**Content angles:**

1. **Demo:** “Turn one hard-coded Django landing page into reusable Wagtail blocks.” Show the editor experience, not only code.
2. **Buyer education:** “What a safe Django-to-Wagtail migration plan looks like in 21 days,” covering Blueprint, priority-content migration, redirects, staging, and rollback.
3. **Editor autonomy:** “Five CMS features that stop marketers needing a developer,” such as page types, reusable blocks, roles, SEO fields, and a publishing guide.

**Cold message:**

> Hi [Name] — I made a short video on [relevant topic] after seeing teams struggle with [pain]. Since [Company] uses Django/has frequent content updates, would you like the link?

**Warm message (past client):**

> Hi [Name] — I’m sharing short practical Django/Wagtail videos for teams managing their own sites. Here’s one on [topic]: [link]. If it is useful, I’d be grateful if you shared it with anyone handling a Django website.

## Cold outreach (email or LinkedIn)

**Posting frequency:** 10 personalized messages/day, Monday-Friday; follow up after 4 business days and again 7 business days later. Target companies with Django job posts, visible Django stacks, stale marketing pages, frequent launches, or agency partners that maintain Django sites.

**Content angles to reference in outreach:**

1. **Content velocity:** “Your team can launch and revise pages without a developer ticket.”
2. **Low-risk migration:** “Preserve the brand, migrate agreed priority content, review on staging, and retain a rollback plan.”
3. **Accountability:** “One Django/Wagtail owner from Blueprint through launch and editor handover.”

**Cold message:**

> Subject: Making [Company]'s Django pages easier to update
>
> Hi [Name] — I noticed [specific trigger]. I help teams turn hard-to-update Django sites into editor-friendly Wagtail CMSs in a focused 21-day launch. Would a short CMS Blueprint idea for [Company] be useful?

**Warm message (past client):**

> Hi [Name] — I’m opening a few Wagtail CMS Launch Sprint slots for Django teams that need faster content updates. Since we worked together on [project], could I ask for one relevant introduction if someone comes to mind?

## Warm outreach (past clients)

**Posting frequency:** 3 personal check-ins/day, Monday, Wednesday, and Friday; never bulk-send. Start with clients who praised quality, speed, communication, problem-solving, or post-launch support.

**Content angles to share:**

1. **New focused offer:** The 21-day Wagtail CMS Launch Sprint for a defined $2,400 scope.
2. **Concrete outcome:** Marketing teams can update representative pages confidently after handover, with reusable content blocks and a publishing guide.
3. **Referral fit:** Ask for introductions to founders, agencies, or marketing teams maintaining Django sites and waiting on developers for content changes.

**Cold message (for an adjacent contact, not a past client):**

> Hi [Name] — [Mutual contact/context] suggested I reach out. I help Django teams remove website-update bottlenecks with focused Wagtail CMS launches. Is content publishing something [Company] is trying to make easier this quarter?

**Warm message (past client):**

> Hi [Name] — I hope things are going well. I’m now focused on 21-day Wagtail CMS launches for Django sites, so marketing teams can update pages without relying on developers. Is that useful for you, or is there one person you’d suggest I speak with?

## Rules that keep this lightweight

- Use one weekly topic across all channels; adapt the format instead of inventing six topics.
- Every post ends with one simple CTA: “Reply `CMS`,” “DM me for the checklist,” or “Want a 3-point CMS Blueprint idea?”
- Personalize outreach with one real trigger; do not send generic AI-written pitches.
- Do not pitch in Reddit comments unless the community permits it and the person asks for help.
- Spend 30 minutes/day on publishing and engagement, 45-60 minutes/day on outreach and follow-up.
