# Daily Income-Rebuild System

## Operating rule

For the next 30 days, the Wagtail CMS Launch Sprint is the primary offer. Use Django/Wagtail maintenance, rescue, and full-stack work as adjacent opportunities. Treat GenAI automation as a relevant add-on only when the client problem calls for it.

Every workday must produce three visible outputs:

1. Qualified proposals sent.
2. Direct conversations started.
3. One piece of proof or useful public content.

Learning is allowed only when it is immediately applied to a proposal, portfolio item, outreach message, or client-ready demo.

## Fixed daily schedule (Monday-Saturday)

| Time | Block | Definition of done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08:30-08:45 | Plan and pipeline review | Choose today's 3 priority jobs/leads; write their next action in the tracker. |
| 08:45-11:15 | Deep work: proposals and paid work | Send **3 tailored Upwork proposals** before doing anything else. If active client work exists, use the remaining time for its highest-value milestone. |
| 11:15-11:45 | Follow-ups and direct outreach | Send **5 targeted messages**: past clients, agency owners, founders, or warm LinkedIn connections. Follow up with leads after 2-3 business days. |
| 11:45-13:00 | Break | Fully away from job feeds and tutorials. |
| 13:00-14:30 | Deep work: sales asset or delivery | Build one concrete asset: Wagtail Sprint case-study section, CMS audit checklist, short Loom demo, portfolio improvement, or client delivery. |
| 14:30-15:00 | Content | Publish or prepare one focused post. On non-publishing days, turn one proposal insight, client problem, or asset into a draft. |
| 15:00-15:30 | Learning: **30-minute hard cap** | Learn one narrow item only. Stop at 30 minutes; record the applied output/link in the tracker. No courses, playlists, or tutorial browsing without an immediate task. |
| 15:30-16:00 | Apply learning + admin | Apply the lesson to a demo, template, proposal, or portfolio. Update the tracker and prepare tomorrow's first three targets. |

**Daily minimum:** 3 proposals, 5 direct outreach/follow-ups, 1 sales asset or delivery block, and tracker updated. If a client deadline needs more time, protect client delivery first, then complete at least 1 proposal and 3 outreach messages.

### Content cadence

Publish **3 times per week** (Monday, Wednesday, Friday). Keep each post tied to an offer-relevant problem:

- A Django site that makes every content edit a developer task.
- What a Wagtail CMS Blueprint prevents before migration starts.
- A before/after walkthrough of reusable editor blocks.
- A practical automation or AI workflow attached to a real internal bottleneck.

End each post with one clear invitation: “Message me if your Django site is slow to update” or “I can review your CMS bottleneck.”

## Weekly checklist (review every Saturday, 16:00)

- [ ] Sent **18+ tailored Upwork proposals** (3 per workday).
- [ ] Sent **30+ direct outreach/follow-up messages** (5 per workday).
- [ ] Published **3 LinkedIn posts**.
- [ ] Created **3 sales assets**: case-study improvement, audit/checklist, Loom, portfolio item, or proposal template.
- [ ] Completed **no more than 180 minutes of learning** total.
- [ ] Applied every learning session to a demo, asset, proposal, or delivery task; list the evidence.
- [ ] Followed up on every open proposal/lead that is due.
- [ ] Reviewed results: replies, calls, interviews, proposals viewed, and revenue; choose one adjustment for next week.

## One-file tracking method

Maintain this single table at the bottom of this file (or copy it into a spreadsheet). One row per workday. Count only completed actions. Keep links short: job URL, LinkedIn post, Loom, asset, or applied-learning output.

| Date | Proposals (goal 3) | Outreach/follow-ups (goal 5) | Content / asset completed | Learning minutes (max 30) | Learning applied to | Replies / calls / revenue | Tomorrow's first 3 targets |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | 0 | 0 |  | 0 |  |  |  |

### Weekly scorecard

At Saturday review, add one line below the daily rows:

`Week of YYYY-MM-DD | Proposals: __/18 | Outreach: __/30 | Posts: __/3 | Assets: __/3 | Learning: __/180 min | Replies: __ | Calls: __ | Revenue: $__ | Next week's one change: ____`

The tracker is for action and feedback, not guilt. If a number misses its goal, identify the exact blocked step (finding leads, writing proposals, following up, or shipping proof) and make that Monday's 08:30 priority.
